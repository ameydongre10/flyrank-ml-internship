## 3. Where Real Captures Beat AI Generation (The Discernment Rule)

When evaluating candidates for technical case studies, **AI generation is an active liability**.

### Proof vs. Illustration
* **Case Study 1 (The Decision Tree Model)**:
  * *The temptation*: Generate an AI concept image of a "futuristic 3D neural brain sorting website articles."
  * *Why real capture won*: Technical interviewers do not hire illustrators; they hire engineers who understand data contracts. A cropped scikit-learn feature importance plot proves we actually trained a tree, eliminated target leakage, and know why `days_with_impressions` drives the split.
* **Case Study 2 (DuckDB 79M Row Telemetry)**:
  * *The temptation*: Generate an AI rendering of a "glowing crystalline server rack with fiber-optic beams."
  * *Why real capture won*: The actual terminal output showing DuckDB's `EXPLAIN ANALYZE` with `142.6 ms execution time` and `79,240,118 rows processed` is impossible to fake and immediately establishes credibility with Staff/Principal engineers.
* **About / Bio**:
  * *The temptation*: Generate an AI portrait using Midjourney or Flux to get perfect cinematic lighting.
  * *Why real photo won*: An AI headshot triggers immediate distrust. If a candidate uses a synthetic face, interviewers question whether the benchmark numbers and code are also synthetic. Real camera capture is non-negotiable.

## 4. Connective Tissue: Consistent Style & Prompt Control

To ensure connective tissue images feel like a unified set rather than an accidental pile, all generated elements were locked to the **Swiss Modernist Identity Kit tokens**:

* **Color Tokens**: `#F8F9FA` (Canvas), `#111215` (Lines), `#2B2F38` (Secondary Structure), `#0D52D6` (Focal Stroke).
* **Grid Scale**: 24px baseline rhythm.
* **Stroke Weight**: Uniform 1.5px structural borders with 2.0px component lines.

### Prompt Control & Iteration (System Glyphs):
* **Prompt Iteration 1 (Too busy)**: `"Set of 4 technology icons for machine learning, database, tree, and shield, 3d render, clay style, soft shadows"`  
  $\rightarrow$ *Outcome*: Looked like a playful consumer mobile app; clashed with our serious technical tone.
* **Prompt Iteration 2 (Too minimal/flat)**: `"Flat black line icons for database, algorithm, list, shield, white background"`  
  $\rightarrow$ *Outcome*: Generic stock clip art; lacked the specific domain language of search intelligence.
* **Prompt Iteration 3 (Kept - Production Asset)**: `"Precision technical vector glyphs, 2px stroke, architectural CAD style, deep slate #2B2F38 with cobalt blue #0D52D6 focal accents, 100x100 square container, Swiss modernist line balance"`  
  $\rightarrow$ *Outcome*: Produced `system_topology_icons.svg` — calibrated, disciplined, and belonging directly to our visual identity kit.

## 5. Ruthless Curation: The Graded Rejection Ledger

> *"Judgment matters more than generation. Knowing what to reject separates an intentional engineer from someone just prompting for novelty."*

### Rejection 01: Conceptual "Neural Brain" Search Illustration
* **Intended Use**: Hero graphic or Lead Case Study thumbnail.
* **Attempted Prompt**: `"Isometric 3D render of a luminous glowing neural network brain analyzing Google search rankings, glassmorphism, floating particle streams, volumetric lighting, Octane render, purple and blue gradients"`
* **Why It Was Rejected (Technical & Aesthetic Verdict)**:
  1. **AI Slop**: Violates Rule 2 of our craftsmanship protocol. Purple/neon volumetric gradients are the most pervasive AI cliché on the web.
  2. **Zero Information Density**: The image conveys zero actual engineering information. It treats machine learning like magic rather than reproducible mathematics.
  3. **Visual Noise**: It shouts louder than the actual case study data. In our identity kit, the design must recede so the case study metrics are the loudest element.

### Rejection 02: Cyberpunk "Hacker Terminal" Data Scan
* **Intended Use**: DuckDB 79M-row warehouse architecture visual.
* **Attempted Prompt**: `"Cyberpunk command line terminal on multi-monitor setup in dark room, green matrix digital rain, complex data streaming graphs, cinematic depth of field"`
* **Why It Was Rejected (Technical & Aesthetic Verdict)**:
  1. **Inauthentic Theatrics**: Data engineers working in SQL and Parquet do not work in green matrix rain. It feels like a Hollywood prop rather than an authentic technical workspace.
  2. **Unreadable Metrics**: The pseudo-code in generated terminal images consists of gibberish characters and warped syntax. A real engineer scanning the portfolio will immediately spot the fake syntax.
  3. **Aesthetic Disharmony**: High-contrast black/green clashes aggressively with our `#F8F9FA` light-mode identity kit.

### Rejection 03: Synthetic "Studio Headshot" Diffusion Avatar
* **Intended Use**: Profile and About section bio image.
* **Attempted Prompt**: `"Photorealistic corporate studio portrait of a 24-year-old male machine learning engineer, sharp suit, subtle confident smile, clean bokeh background, 85mm lens, f/1.8, high-end editorial lighting"`
* **Why It Was Rejected (Technical & Aesthetic Verdict)**:
  1. **Uncanny Valley**: Synthetic skin diffusion has an unmistakable plastic waxiness around the forehead and jawline, and the catchlights in the pupils are unnaturally symmetrical.
  2. **Integrity Trap**: A portfolio is a proof of capability and personal accountability. Presenting a fabricated likeness immediately plants doubts about whether the author wrote the code or whether the project is also a hallucination. Real photography wins every time.
